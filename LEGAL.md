## Data Sources

### FIA Stewards' Decision PDFs

FIA stewards' decision documents are **public documents** published by the
Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile on fia.com/documents. RACEJUDGE:

- Downloads and indexes these documents for **search, research, and educational purposes**
- Does not sell or redistribute the original PDFs
- Links back to the original FIA source URL for every document
- Respects the FIA's robots.txt (rate-limit: 1 request / 5 seconds)

Applicable doctrine: public records indexing, transformative use for
journalistic and educational commentary.

### OpenF1 Data

Race control messages, team radio clips, and telemetry are sourced from the
OpenF1 public API ([openf1.org](https://openf1.org)), available under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribution is provided in all data exports.

### Team Radio Audio

Team radio clips are fetched from OpenF1's recording URLs. RACEJUDGE:

- Does **not** host audio files beyond a temporary local cache for transcription
- Uploads clips to a **private** R2 bucket, not publicly accessible
- Does not redistribute raw audio to end users
- Displays only transcripts (text) in the public UI, never playable audio

### F1TV / FOM Video

RACEJUDGE stores **reference URLs only** to officially published F1TV content.
We do not host, download, redistribute, or bypass authentication for FOM video.

## DMCA / Copyright Takedown Procedure

If you believe RACEJUDGE has infringed your copyright, send written notice to:

**Email:** `legal@racejudge.io` *(until launch: `maruteymani31@gmail.com`)*

Your notice must include:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed
2. Identification of the infringing material in RACEJUDGE (URL or doc_id)
3. Your contact information (name, address, telephone, email)
4. A good-faith statement that the use is not authorised by the rights holder
5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate under penalty of perjury
6. Your physical or electronic signature

On receipt of a valid DMCA notice we will:

- Remove or disable the identified material within **48 hours**
- Notify the uploader where applicable
- Provide a counter-notice mechanism for disputed removals
