# Legal & Compliance

## Media Rights

All FIA Stewards' Decision PDFs are public documents published at fia.com/documents.

F1 onboard video and team radio audio are FOM-licensed content. RACEJUDGE:
- Links only to FOM's officially-published clip URLs (F1TV embeds)
- Caches only audio from OpenF1's already-public `recording_url` endpoints (published by FOM through the broadcast feed)
- Never hosts unlicensed video copies

## DMCA / Takedown

To request removal of content: email **legal@racejudge.com** with:
1. Description of the copyrighted work
2. URL of the infringing content on racejudge.com
3. Your contact information and a statement of good faith

We will respond within 48 hours and remove content within 5 business days of a valid notice.
