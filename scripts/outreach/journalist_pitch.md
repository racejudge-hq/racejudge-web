## Email Template — Version A (data angle)

**Subject:** A tool that answers "Is this penalty consistent?" — looking for design partners

Hi [Name],

I'm building RACEJUDGE — a searchable, structured database of every FIA Stewards' Decision from 2018 to today, cross-linked to race control messages and telemetry.

**The core problem it solves:** Your December 2025 stewarding deep-dive [or: "stories like the Zandvoort/Sainz overturn"] had to rely on hand-disclosed FIA figures because no structured database existed. RACEJUDGE changes that.

Here's what it does:

- **Precedent search:** Natural-language queries like "Show me every 10-second penalty for forcing a car off track on corner exit since 2022" — results in under a second.
- **Penalty predictor:** Given an incident description, returns a calibrated probability distribution over outcomes (NFA, reprimand, 5s, 10s, drive-through, DSQ) with cited guideline articles.
- **Live mode:** When OpenF1 emits "UNDER INVESTIGATION" during a session, it auto-pushes the top-5 most similar historical incidents to subscribers within ~5 seconds.
- **Consistency heat-maps:** Statistical analysis of penalty severity by infraction type, season, steward panel, and circuit — flags decisions that are statistically anomalous.

It's built on public data: FIA decision PDFs (which are public documents), OpenF1 race control + telemetry, and the Penalty Guidelines the FIA published in June 2025.

**What I'm looking for:** 2–3 journalists as design partners before public launch. In exchange for early access and feedback, I'll give you:
- Private preview access
- Advance export of the full 2024–25 structured incident dataset (CSV)
- First notification when the tool goes live

The tool isn't live yet — I'm 4 weeks from a working search prototype. But I wanted to get your input on what would actually be useful at the trackside before I build the wrong thing.

Would you be open to a 30-minute call or an async exchange via email?

[Your name]
